## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a premier institutional identity for strategic development, organizational innovation, and enterprise transformation within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. The aesthetic balances Saudi visionary leadership, intellectual rigor, and modern institutional agility.

### Aesthetic Movement: Warm Institutional Modernism
The design system fuses corporate architectural discipline with warm, human-centered warmth. It translates Saudi Vision 2030’s ambitious strategic momentum into visual form by contrasting sovereign midnight tones with vibrant solar amber and saffron accents.

- **Trust & Authority:** Structured layouts, authoritative geometric forms, and high-contrast typographic balance communicate stability, discretion, and national-scale impact.
- **Creativity & Ingenuity:** Radiant warm gradients, ambient luminous halos, and fluid pill-shaped indicators signify dynamic innovation, foresight, and adaptive solutions.
- **Cultural Grounding & RTL Primacy:** Crafted natively for Right-to-Left (RTL) reading flows, respecting Arabic calligraphic balance and reading metrics without appearing retrofitted from Western frameworks.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative corporate baseline energized by optimistic warmth:

- **Primary (`#0F172A` / `#0A0D17`):** Midnight Obsidian. Anchors high-level leadership cards, formal reports, and authoritative institutional narratives.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B` / `#D97706`):** Solar Amber. Represents intellect, strategic breakthroughs, and executive decisions. Used for primary CTA buttons, milestone markers, active states, and radiant focal points.
- **Tertiary (`#1E1B4B`):** Deep Regal Indigo. Imparts strategic depth in dark-themed feature cards, hero backdrops, and secondary corporate accents.
- **Surfaces (`#FFFDF9` / `#F8FAFC`):** Sand Silk and Warm Alabaster. Replaces sterile cool whites with soft, premium warmth that complements Arabic typography and executive dashboards.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#F3E8D6` light / `#2E384D` dark):** Low-contrast structural frames that softly separate content cards without visual clutter.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes Arabic reading harmony, balancing high institutional legibility with contemporary executive flair.

- **Font Family:** `IBM Plex Sans` (Arabic) serves as the master typeface across all levels, bringing architectural clarity, open counters, and precise vertical proportion.
- **RTL Considerations:** Line heights are calibrated 15–20% higher than standard Latin baselines (`lineHeight: 1.75rem` for 1rem text) to provide room for Arabic diacritics and ligatures without clipping.
- **Hierarchical Balance:** Executive titles utilize Bold (700) and SemiBold (600) weights, avoiding synthetic heavy weights to preserve optical clarity. Numerical metrics and indicators use SemiBold and Bold numerals with tabular alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model combines an executive grid system with generous breathing room suited for dual-density institutional dashboards and editorial public sites.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with `margin-lg` (5rem/80px) page edges and `gutter-lg` (2rem/32px).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** 8-column layout with `margin-md` (3rem/48px) and `gutter` (1.5rem/24px).
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** 4-column layout with `margin` (1.5rem/24px) side safety padding and `gutter-sm` (1rem/16px).

### Spacing Principles
- **Asymmetric Content Framing:** Strategic sections alternate between full-width immersive midnight blocks and inset warm cream cards.
- **Section Breaks:** Major narrative milestones utilize `space-2xl` for executive pacing, avoiding dense enterprise clutter.
- **RTL Alignment Logic:** Primary visual anchors, icons, and list indicators align strictly to the right margin, with flow directing leftward.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm ambient lighting, dual-tone card containers, and luminous innovation halos rather than cold drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels

1. **Flat Base (Level 0):** Used on canvas backgrounds (`#FFFDF9`) and structural dividers. Zero shadow.
2. **Subtle Executive Tint (Level 1):** Inset content cards on light surfaces. 
   - Border: `1px solid #F3E8D6`
   - Shadow: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.03)`
3. **Elevated Floating Card (Level 2):** Primary cards, interactive modules, and segmented counters.
   - Shadow: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.06)`
4. **Nocturne Depth (Dark Cards):** Cards utilizing `#0F172A` or `#1E1B4B` surfaces.
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
   - Ambient Glow: `0px 20px 40px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4), inset 0px 1px 0px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`
5. **Solar Innovation Halo (Hero CTAs & Active Badges):**
   - Amber Shadow: `0px 10px 25px -3px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`

## Shapes

The design system uses a pill-shaped and rounded-geometry system (`level 3`). This balances corporate authority with approachable modernity:

- **Executive Cards & Modules:** Styled with generous `rounded-3xl` radii (24px to 32px), creating smooth, modern containers that frame institutional content.
- **Buttons, Badges & Chips:** Completely pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / 9999px), providing high touchability and friendly modern contrast against structured grid columns.
- **Inner Micro-Elements:** Form inputs, segmented count badges, and icon frames adopt soft curved corners (`rounded-xl` / 12px to 16px).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Solar Innovation Button):** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), rendered with a directional warm gradient from `#F59E0B` to `#EA580C`. White text (`#FFFFFF`) in SemiBold weight. Includes subtle RTL leading icon (right-side) and soft warm glow on hover (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Corporate Button:** Outlined or solid midnight surface (`#0F172A`), white text, accompanied by an amber accent dot or icon.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background, midnight navy text, warm amber hover underline or micro-pill background fill on interaction.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with horizontal padding (`px-4 py-1.5`).
- **Institutional Sub-Tag:** Soft warm cream background (`#FEF3C7`), deep amber text (`#D97706`), with an optional miniature amber indicator dot preceding the Arabic text.
- **Nocturne Badge:** Translucent white border and fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`) over deep navy backdrops.

### Metric & Segmented Counters
- Distinct numbered badges used in methodology flows (e.g., 01, 02, 03).
- Contained within rounded squircle or circular badges (`w-12 h-12`) featuring gradient or tonal amber fill with bold, crisp Arabic numerals.
- Displayed prominently at the upper-right corner of step cards in RTL layouts.

### Cards & Institutional Tiles
- **Light Insight Card:** `#FFFFFF` or `#FFFDF9` surface with `1px solid #F3E8D6` border, `rounded-3xl` corners, and ambient warm shadow.
- **Vision Midnight Card:** `#0F172A` to `#1E1B4B` subtle gradient surface, white typography, warm amber micro-accents, and inset top-border highlights (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).

### Input Fields & Forms
- Generous padding (`py-3.5 px-4`), `rounded-2xl` geometry, warm off-white surface (`#F8FAFC`), and subtle border (`#E2E8F0`).
- Focus state activates an amber border (`#F59E0B`) with a diffused 3px focus ring (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`).
- Labels are positioned strictly right-aligned with an accompanying required indicator in terracotta amber.

### Navigation Header
- Floating glassmorphic dock or sticky pill bar with backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md`), bordered with `rgba(243, 232, 214, 0.8)`.
- Features institutional insignia on the right (RTL), centered navigation links with amber active indicators, and quick-action consultation button on the far left.
