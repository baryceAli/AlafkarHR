# Stakeholder Register

| Stakeholder | Interest | Responsibilities | Engagement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Executive Sponsor | Business outcome and go-live approval. | Resolve escalations, approve scope, approve go-live. | Steering committee and milestone sign-offs. |
| HR Sponsor | HR process readiness. | Approve HR workflows, data standards, training approach. | Weekly workstream review. |
| HR Administrator | Day-to-day HR operations. | Validate employee setup, lifecycle, documents, skills, reports. | Discovery, UAT, training. |
| Payroll Lead | Payroll accuracy and statutory compliance. | Approve payroll configuration, test salary runs, WPS, payslips. | Payroll workshops, parallel run, sign-off. |
| Attendance Lead | Time and attendance operations. | Validate shifts, roster, attendance sessions, requests, corrections, reports. | Attendance workshops and UAT. |
| IT Security Lead | User access and support. | Create users, roles, permissions, branch access, support handover. | Configuration and security testing. |
| Department Managers | Team approval workflows. | Validate team visibility, approvals, reports, manager guide. | UAT and manager training. |
| Employees | Self-service usage. | Use attendance and leave workflows accurately. | Training and go-live communications. |
| Recruiter | Hiring workflow. | Validate staffing plans, requisitions, applicants, interviews, offers. | Recruitment validation workshop. |
| Performance Owner | Appraisal workflow. | Validate cycles, goals, competencies, reviews, approvals. | Performance validation workshop. |
| Training Coordinator | Training operations. | Validate programs, events, attendees, results, certificates. | Training validation workshop. |
| Finance Reviewer | Payroll accounting and cost review. | Validate payroll posting and financial review process. | Payroll/finance testing. |
| Implementation Lead | Delivery ownership. | Manage plan, risks, issues, documents, sign-offs. | Daily delivery coordination. |

## Engagement Principles

- Decisions must have a named business owner.
- Data owners must approve data before migration.
- Key users must test the workflows they will rely on after go-live.
- Open issues must be ranked by go-live impact, not only by technical complexity.
