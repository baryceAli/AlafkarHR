# Support and Hypercare Plan

## Hypercare Duration

Recommended duration: two weeks after go-live, extendable if payroll or attendance issues remain open.

## Support Channels

| Channel | Purpose | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hypercare chat/channel | Fast triage for go-live issues. | Support Lead |
| Issue register | Formal tracking, severity, owner, status. | Implementation Lead |
| HR helpdesk | Employee process questions. | HR Admin |
| IT helpdesk | Login, role, access, browser, environment issues. | IT Lead |
| Payroll support | Payroll run and payslip issues. | Payroll Lead |

## Triage Categories

| Category | Examples | First owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access | Login, missing menu, wrong role, branch visibility. | IT Lead |
| Employee data | Wrong department, missing document, incorrect status. | HR Admin |
| Attendance | Check-in issue, shift mismatch, exception approval. | Attendance Officer |
| Leave | Balance issue, application status, attachment problem. | HR Admin |
| Payroll | Contract, component, run, payslip, loan, WPS issue. | Payroll Lead |
| Training/process | User does not know how to complete task. | HR/Implementation |

## SLA During Hypercare

| Severity | Response target | Resolution target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | 1 business hour | Same day or workaround |
| High | 4 business hours | 2 business days |
| Medium | 1 business day | 5 business days |
| Low | 2 business days | Planned backlog |

## Daily Hypercare Agenda

1. Review new issues.
2. Confirm severity and owner.
3. Review blockers for HR, attendance, leave, and payroll.
4. Confirm user communication needs.
5. Close resolved issues with business confirmation.

## Handover to Steady-State Support

Hypercare can close when:

- Critical/high issues are closed or accepted.
- Support team can handle common access and process questions.
- Updated role guides or FAQs are published.
- Open backlog has owners and target dates.
