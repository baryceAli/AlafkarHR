# Training Plan

## Training Objectives

- Prepare each role to perform daily AlafkarHR work without relying on the implementation team.
- Explain data ownership, approval responsibilities, and escalation paths.
- Make role-based user guides the primary reference after go-live.

## Training Tracks

| Track | Audience | Topics | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| System Administration | IT administrators | Login, users, roles, permissions, branch access, general settings, support. | 1 day |
| HR Administration | HR team | Organization masters, employees, lifecycle, documents, skills, leave setup, reports. | 2 days |
| Attendance | Attendance officers and managers | Shifts, sessions, holidays, requests, approvals, reports, roster. | 1 day |
| Payroll | Payroll team and finance reviewers | Components, contracts, structures, periods, loans, payroll entries, payslips, WPS, Saudi payroll, accounting posting. | 2 days |
| Employee Self-Service | Employees | My attendance, leave applications, emergency leave, basic profile/reference behavior. | 1-2 hours |
| Manager Self-Service | Managers/supervisors | Team review, approvals, attendance/leave exceptions, reports. | Half day |
| Talent | Recruitment, performance, training owners | Requisitions/applicants/offers, appraisals, training events. | 1 day |
| Executive | Leadership | Dashboards, reports, interpretation, escalation. | 1 hour |

## Training Materials

- Role guides in this documentation package.
- UAT scripts converted to practice exercises.
- Company-specific policy notes.
- Go-live support contacts.
- Known limitations and business validation decisions.

## Training Readiness Checklist

- [ ] Training environment or configured production preview is available.
- [ ] Demo users exist for each role.
- [ ] Sample employee and payroll data are prepared.
- [ ] User guides are reviewed by business owners.
- [ ] Attendance tracking sheet is ready.
- [ ] Feedback form is ready.

## Training Completion Criteria

- Administrators complete role and data setup exercises.
- HR administrators complete employee and leave exercises.
- Payroll officers complete at least one payroll run exercise.
- Managers complete approval exercises.
- Employees receive self-service training or communications.
