Why Annual Sweeps Matter

Creosote — the tar-like residue wood smoke leaves behind — is fuel sitting inside your chimney. An eighth of an inch is enough to sustain a flue fire.

1 in 4home heating fires start in chimneys and flues (NFPA)
  • Sweep annually if you burn more than one cord per season
  • Inspect before buying a home or after any chimney fire
  • Cap every flue — water damage costs more than sweeping ever will
  • Burn seasoned hardwood only; wet wood triples creosote
